The resolved quality policy (`ResolvedSubmissionQualityPolicy`) combining profile config and command-line quality mode. Controls linguistic audit severity, target-language rules, self-review mode, grammar backend selection, and fingerprinting. Shared between translation acceptance and grammar-judge workflows to prevent policy drift.
